---
title: "EQ that clears space"
---

<p>EQ is mostly about removing what each sound doesn't need, so the important part of every other sound comes through. Cut narrow and boost wide.</p>
<EqChart />
<Steps>
  <li><div><b>High-pass anything that isn't bass or kick.</b> Pads, vocals and textures usually start around 100–150 Hz. Sweep up until it thins, then back off a little.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Find a resonance with a narrow boost.</b> In EQ Eight, raise one band by about 10 dB with a high Q and sweep slowly. Where it rings or honks, cut 3–6 dB instead.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Boost wide and gently.</b> A broad 1–2 dB shelf for air above 10 kHz does more than a sharp 6 dB peak.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Judge at matched loudness.</b> Use EQ Eight's Output so bypass sounds the same level.</div></li>
</Steps>
<Task>Clean the Tape Pad: high-pass it, find and cut one resonance, then compare against bypass at matched loudness.</Task>
