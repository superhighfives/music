title: "Space: reverb and delay"
---

<p>Put reverb and delay on return tracks, not on each track, so every sound shares one room. That shared room is most of what makes a mix feel glued.</p>

<Steps>
  <li><div><b>Returns fully wet.</b> Dry/Wet at 100% on every return; the send knob sets how much.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>EQ the returns.</b> High-pass reverbs around 250 Hz so the low end stays clear.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Pre-delay keeps things close.</b> A short gap before the tail keeps vocals and hits in front.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Automate the sends,</b> not the reverb. Pushing a send for one phrase is how a song breathes.</div></li>
</Steps>
<Task>Build the three returns and mix a loop using only send levels. No reverb on individual tracks.</Task>
