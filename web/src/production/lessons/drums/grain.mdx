title: "Snares and hats from noise"
---

<p>Stock hats sit on top of a mix. Slices of your own textures sound like they belong to the record.</p>
<Sheet head="Hats">
  <Row>
    <Cell l="Source" tone="cool">City or rain, sliced</Cell>
    <Cell l="Velocity" num>40 – 70</Cell>
    <Cell l="Chance" num>60% on offbeats</Cell>
    <Cell l="Swing" num>16th · 55–60%</Cell>
  </Row>
  <Row>
    <Cell l="Feel" span={2}>Hats at <span class="mono"><V k="doubleBpm" /></span>, backbeat at <span class="mono"><V k="bpm" /></span></Cell>
    <Cell l="High-pass" num span={2}>400 Hz</Cell>
  </Row>
</Sheet>
<Steps>
  <li><div><b>Slice a texture into 16</b> in Simpler's Slice mode and keep the brightest four.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Layer a snare:</b> body from a sample, grain from a 100 ms texture slice.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Lower the Chance</b> on every other hat so the pattern breathes.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Drag a 16th swing</b> from the Groove Pool.</div></li>
</Steps>
<Task>Program hats at double time over a half-time backbeat. Add Chance and swing until it breathes.</Task>
