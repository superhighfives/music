---

<p>At slow tempos a kick rings for a whole beat, so a wrong pitch clashes with the bass for that long. Tune it.</p>
<Sheet head="Kick" sub="Pad C1">
  <Row>
    <Cell l="Sample">Kick 49 Hz (G1)</Cell>
    <Cell l="Root" num tone="lit"><V k="kickRoot" /></Cell>
    <Cell l="Or the fifth" num><V k="kickFifth" /></Cell>
    <Cell l="Note" num><V k="kickNote" /></Cell>
  </Row>
  <Row>
    <Cell l="Decay">Ends before the next ⅛</Cell>
    <Cell l="⅛ note" num><V k="eighth" /></Cell>
    <Cell l="Saturation">Decapitator · A · 3</Cell>
    <Cell l="Sends">None, keep it dry</Cell>
  </Row>
</Sheet>
<Steps>
  <li><div><b>Tune to the root</b> shown above, or the fifth if the root disappears on small speakers.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Shorten the tail</b> until it stops before the next eighth note.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Add weight with saturation, not EQ,</b> so it reads on laptop speakers.</div></li>
</Steps>
<Task>Tune the kick to the root and the fifth. Keep the one that sits better under the bass.</Task>
