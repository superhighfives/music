<p>The shape that works for short pieces: the weather first, then the harmony, then the pulse. Take it away before the end.</p>
<Arrangement />
<Steps>
  <li><div><b>Bars 1–8, fog.</b> One bed loop, filtered dark, opening slowly.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Bars 9–16, pad.</b> The progression arrives, one chord every two bars.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Bars 17–32, pulse.</b> Kick and bass together, hats four bars later.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Bars 33–36, strip back.</b> Drums and bass out, space up.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Bars 37–40, last swell.</b> Everything back.</div></li>
  <li><div><b>Bars 41–44, tail.</b> Fade the bed; don't cut it.</div></li>
</Steps>
